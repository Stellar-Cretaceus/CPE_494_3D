## Concept
"Solnar Child", A young angel, a celestial being of pure light, has been captured and imprisoned within the crumbling walls of a desecrated, blood-red cathedral. Once a place of worship, the church is now a sprawling, desolate labyrinth that defies the laws of space—an endless corridor of ruin where the exit never appears.   
The Protagonist: The "Solnar Child," an angel who carries the dual essence of the cosmos within her:
   - The Blessing of the Sun (Solar): A fierce offensive power that allows her to manifest orbs of radiant heat to shatter the obstacles in her path.
   - The Grace of the Moon (Luna): A protective aura that grants her divine immunity, allowing her to phase through danger unscathed.

## Feature 
- SHOOT: Player can SHOOT the huge obstacle that blocks our way. Note that we can shoot when we are not jumping, so decide carefully.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c32b8b76-efb8-4015-81e7-04bd85ef0eda

- JUMP: Player can JUMP to evade the lower obstacle.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e365b49e-13fd-4138-a32e-764efddac0a8

- ENDLESS RUN: Survive as long as you can; the game will continuously loop itself, with rock types randomly spawn. 
- ONE LIFE: The game ends when you collide. You only have one life. The Game will restart if the player collides. 
  

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/41f8bd96-1f9b-4ff1-a498-c312af4b511a


- ITEMS: Collide with ITEMS to game powerups. There are two items: "Solar Spear" and "Lunar Shield."
- SOLAR MODE: Automatically fires bullets for a duration of 10 secs.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/67d3ede3-aff1-4704-a15a-4b67c1a26117


- LUNAR MODE: Grants a shield to the player, becoming immune to rocks for 10 secs.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7feb1564-3f17-4394-974e-621c3baa270d
